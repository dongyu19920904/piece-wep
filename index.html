About Lumina

Lumina was founded in 2018 with a mission to transform everyday spaces through innovative lighting technology. Our team of engineers, designers, and lighting experts work together to create products that are not only functional but also beautiful.

We believe that lighting is fundamental to how we experience our environments, affecting everything from our mood and productivity to our health and wellbeing. That's why we're committed to developing solutions that give people complete control over their lighting experience.
